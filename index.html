Key Findings

Genre Dominance

Pop and Hip Hop lead streaming charts with the highest average popularity scores across all analyzed playlists.

Hit Formula

High energy + high danceability tracks are significantly more likely to hit viral status (80+ popularity score).

Listener Sentiment

YouTube comments average positive sentiment. K-Pop videos show the highest engagement and comment rates per view.

Tempo Sweet Spot

Hits across all genres cluster around 100–130 BPM — tempo consistency cuts across stylistic boundaries.
